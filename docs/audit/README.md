## Coverage and limits

The raw sessions are not published. Thirteen video requests have complete saved request/result receipts. The earlier video 7 has its exact prompt and output recovered from fal Assets, but the complete original input settings are not claimed.

The user prompts are an edited selection, and later financial-platform work is outside this archive's visual-production scope. Duplicated inherited subagent history is not counted as a separate generation.

The audit checked the original scripts and final deck configuration, extracted comparison frames, and smoke-rendered one hero frame and one night frame from the saved Blender scenes. It did not rerender the complete animations or watch every historical video frame-by-frame. Past reported costs are conversation evidence, not a new invoice audit or a promise about future prices. Production slide 1 is directly traced.
